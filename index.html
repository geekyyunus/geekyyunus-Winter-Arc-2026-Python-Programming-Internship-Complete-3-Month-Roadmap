Attendance, Conduct & Work Expectations

  • Attend scheduled classes, mentor meetings, reviews and presentations punctually; the official group will announce session timing and any changes.
  • Complete assigned tasks and submit work by the announced deadlines. If a genuine issue arises, inform the mentor/team early rather than silently missing a deadline.
  • Maintain professional, respectful communication and check official WhatsApp/email announcements regularly.
  • Submit original work. Any permitted external resources or AI assistance must be used responsibly; interns should understand and be able to explain their submitted code.
  • Attendance, participation, task completion, project quality, communication, conduct and assessments may be considered in evaluating progress and programme completion.
  • Repeated absence, uncommunicated delays, incomplete work or failure to follow instructions may affect the assessment and completion status, subject to applicable programme rules.

Programme Policies & Clarifications

  • Dates are based on a planned start of 22 October 2026 and a three-month period ending 21 January 2027. The institute will confirm any changes through official channels.
  • The 90-day roadmap is a structured plan, not a guarantee that a live class or new task will be issued every calendar day. Workload and meetings may vary by phase and project needs.
  • The programme is online. Students should follow the official session links and instructions shared by the internship team.
  • Completion, certificates and any recognition are subject to applicable programme criteria, satisfactory performance, required submissions and institute approval.
  • The internship does not guarantee employment, placement or stipend. Any compensation or other terms, if applicable, are governed only by official written communication.
  • Project examples and tools are indicative. The mentor will confirm the actual project scope, tools, acceptance criteria and deadlines based on feasibility and learning progress.
